# Scientific Dossier: *Umbrachoir lyticus*

**Common name:** Black Choir Virus  
**Ecosystem role:** Virus - Colony rhythm parasite

## Profile

A matte-black polyhedral particle surrounded by vibrating filaments. This cryptid shares the same deep-sea hydrothermal-vent ecosystem as *Hydrosaltator amictus*.

- **Habitat:** dark mineral films shared by vent colonies
- **Type:** Virus
- **Role:** Colony rhythm parasite

## Symbiotic relationships

Infects abandoned film colonies and releases nutrients for Mycocladus; outbreaks disrupt local signals.

## Field behavior

Makes every infected colony click one note off-key, apparently for comedy.
