# Companion Profile: *Sulfuribacillus thermophilus*

**Common name:** Emberspit Microbe  
**Role:** Archaea harvester for *Hydrosaltator amictus*

## Scientific summary

*Sulfuribacillus thermophilus* is a heat-loving sulfur harvester that occupies the warmest channels of the HydroSalts symbiotic matrix. Its mineral-rich biofilm captures reduced sulfur compounds and passes stabilized energy packages to the host colony.

- **Preferred zone:** 65-95 degrees C vent margins
- **Form:** Short rod-like cells in orange mineral films
- **Primary resource:** Dissolved sulfur compounds
- **Exchange:** Chemical energy and heat tolerance for sugars and protected matrix space

The Emberspit colony is recognized by orange flecks in the matrix and a habit of “spitting” harmless sulfur-rich bubbles whenever the host changes direction.
